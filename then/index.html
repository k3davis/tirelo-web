Rolling calendar & tasks for Android

See what's due this month and what's coming, and let finished things set up what comes next.

Coming soon to Google Play

What it does

  • A timeline by monthTasks and decisions sit in the month they're due, on an exact day or just "sometime in March". Overdue things stay in view until you deal with them.
  • Decisions, not just tasks"Renew or let it expire?" Answer when you're ready, or put it off with Later.
  • Next steps that set themselves upFinish a task and its follow-up appears. Repeat a chore after each time you do it. Every change is kept in History, and you can undo what a rule did.
  • Reminders and a home-screen widgetNotifications when you want them, and the next few things at a glance.
  • Yours, offlineNo account and no cloud. Everything stays on your phone. Back up to a file you keep, and restore it any time.
