Why "Tirelo"?

Tirelo means service in Setswana, a language spoken in southern Africa. It was my home for a time, and a place where I used technology in service to others.

I'm a native New Yorker living in Texas, and a Chicagoan at heart, and I still love using technology to meet the needs of others.
